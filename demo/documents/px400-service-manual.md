# PX-400 Service Manual

## Fault E-217 — Encoder signal loss

E-217 indicates that the motion controller lost a valid encoder signal during an indexed movement. Before replacing components, inspect the encoder connector, harness routing, shield termination and visible cable damage. Reseat the connector with power isolated according to the service procedure.

Run Diagnostic D-17 after inspection. If D-17 passes repeatedly and no physical fault is found, capture the controller log and compare the fault timestamp with recent firmware or maintenance changes.

## Replacement guidance

Replace the encoder assembly only after connector and harness inspection or a failed D-17 diagnostic supports component replacement. Record the diagnostic result in the service work order.
