# Northstar Foods Maintenance Agreement

## Covered equipment

The agreement covers the PX-400 packaging system installed on Line 4 at Northstar Foods for corrective on-site service during the active agreement term.

## Corrective service

Corrective service includes technician travel, diagnostic labor and standard troubleshooting for covered equipment. Replacement parts are covered when the failed component is within the listed covered assemblies and the service record includes the diagnostic basis for replacement.

Customer-caused physical damage and non-approved modifications require separate authorization.
