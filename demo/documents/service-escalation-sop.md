# Service Escalation SOP

## Before dispatch

For repeated machine stops, review the last 90 days of service cases, identify recent software or maintenance changes, verify the active maintenance agreement, and check stock for parts named by the relevant troubleshooting procedure.

## Escalation brief

The brief should separate observed facts, documented guidance, unresolved questions and proposed work. A service work order may be proposed by an engineer but requires manager approval before dispatch when it includes replacement parts or customer-site work.
